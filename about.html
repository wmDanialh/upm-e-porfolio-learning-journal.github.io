A NOTE TO THE READER

About this journal

Not just a record of what I did, but a record of how I learned.

Why I started writing

Starting a degree is a new chapter, and I want to remember more than just deadlines and results. This website is my personal space to document the experiences that shape my journey in Computer and Communication Systems Engineering at Universiti Putra Malaysia.

Some entries will be about concepts I finally understand. Others may be about assignments, practical sessions, tests, friendships, uncertainty or days that do not go according to plan.

What you will find here

  • My Journal: dated entries about the semester, arranged chronologically.
  • Learning Log: topics, concepts and technical skills I am working to understand.
  • Gallery: photographs and visual memories from university life.
  • Reflections: honest thoughts about challenges, progress and what I want to improve.

The idea behind it

This is not meant to be a perfect success story. It is a record of the process: trying, making mistakes, asking questions, practising and continuing to grow.
